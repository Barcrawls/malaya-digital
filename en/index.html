Interventions (July 2026)
  • Domain canonicalization (www → clean domain) + sitemap verification
  • Conditional noindex and 301 redirects on indexed junk pages
  • AggregateRating schema repair (Judge.me): 5 valid rich snippets, 0 GSC errors
  • Titles and meta descriptions rewritten against real queries from the performance report
  • Private crawls page rebuilt: heading hierarchy, FAQ ×10, internal links
  • Pillar article + support cluster (75-word direct answer, FAQ, real author, own photo)
  • Duplicate content consolidated with 301 redirects
  • GBP hardened (categories, services, UTM-tagged posts) + Bing Places + Apple Business Connect
Takeaways
  • Technical fixes precede content: schema and consolidation unlocked channels no article alone would have produced
  • Rewriting titles against real queries is the highest immediate-return lever: CTR nearly doubled with no position change
  • Discovery content changed the nature of the channel: from capturing existing demand to generating new demand
  • Data reveals strategy, not just performance: the by-country report now informs the brand's content and partnerships

Let me tell you how we got here…

I've spent nearly a decade running tourism and experience businesses. And for years I assumed the digital side of my businesses was handled — the site worked, bookings came in, what else was there to check?

Until one day I decided to audit my own site thoroughly. What I found surprised me: invisible technical errors, basics missing, opportunities quietly slipping away every single day. And that's when I understood something that changed my approach: when you don't know about these things, you don't even know you have the problem.
